In this video, Hussain explains Muse, Meta's new personal AI agent, which does not only answer questions but carries out real tasks. He frames the whole idea in one line: Muse is not a chatbot that talks about your tasks, it is an agent that finishes them, and your job shifts from asking questions to reviewing what it did.

## Chatbot versus agent

A chatbot hands you an answer, and the work is still yours. The video's example is flights to Miami: a chatbot gives you a list. An agent takes the same request and runs it. It plans the steps, browses the sites, pays with a secure card, and keeps working after you close the app. That last point, background work, is what separates an agent from a conversation that ends when you stop typing.

## What gives it reach

To act, Muse needs access. The video says it connects to Facebook and Instagram and to outside apps such as Spotify, OpenTable and your email. Every app you link is one more place it can act for you, so the advice is to link deliberately.

## Four steps to use it

1. Get access. The video says it is rolling out in the U.S. on iPhone, Android, the web, WhatsApp and a Mac app, with Meta's AI glasses coming later.
2. Connect only the apps you want it to act in.
3. Give it a task in plain language.
4. Let it work, then review what it did.

## What a task looks like

The example is typing a request to book a table for two on Friday at 7:00. The agent plans the job, checks OpenTable, finds a table and holds it, then comes back to you. Anything important waits for your approval: a booking, a payment, or an email sent in your name.

## Three things to check every time

- **Payments.** Check the amount and the merchant before any money moves.
- **Emails.** Read the draft that goes out under your name.
- **Background work.** Because the agent keeps going after you close the app, look at what it actually did.

## The verdict

Hussain's recommendation is to start with low-stakes errands such as music, reservations and drafts, and to link only the apps a task needs. Every payment should be treated as a review rather than a formality. The summary line is that the agent does the legwork while the decisions stay with you.
