## A skill is a folder

This kind of asset building runs on a skill, a folder Claude reads before it works. Inside are three files: a readme, a quick reference, and the SKILL.md that spells out the process. Claude reads it and then follows it. As the video puts it, the file is the whole program.

## How it runs

The steps run in order, and nothing runs out of sequence unless a step itself says so. Claude reads the file and works through each step exactly as written. It gathers your deal context, generates the asset, and returns it ready to share.

## Exactly four asset types

The skill covers exactly four asset types: landing pages, decks, one-pagers, and workflow demos. Each one is shaped by your deal context rather than invented from scratch. That context is your prospect, your audience, and your goal. If you ask for a fifth kind of asset, no format covers it. The video's summary is that create-an-asset does not invent a generic deck from nothing; it builds one of four sales formats shaped by your actual deal.
