A renewal request is only useful if the reviewer can check it quickly. This video, narrated by Liam in for Bear, explains how to ask for a Humanitarians AI fellowship renewal so that the reviewer can verify it in minutes instead of digging through folders. The short version: write a Markdown report in your own fellows folder showing completed work, actual weekly hours and a direct link to the evidence for every claim, then email the report link to hr@humanitarians.ai and bear@humanitarians.ai.

## The trap of expecting the reviewer to reconstruct your work

Professor Bear is very busy and will not go hunting for evidence. A folder of files does not explain what you did, and writing a clear, verifiable report is part of the fellow's job. The video reframes the question. The useful one is not how to ask but what the reviewer can actually verify.

## A vague request hands over the work

The example fellow has a folder full of material, but the renewal message says only "please extend, my work is on GitHub." The work may exist, yet the request has handed the reviewer the job of finding and explaining it.

The video follows one claim to its evidence. The fellow says they prepared an explainer about plug-in components. Opening the linked item shows a beat sheet, which is a script and production plan and not a finished film. There is no viewing link, so the report must say plainly that the video evidence is missing.

## Hours need a record too

The same applies to hours. The example lists 20 hours for each of four weeks, and the video states these are fictional teaching data. A real fellow records actual dates, tasks and time, and connects that record to the work. A video cannot prove 20 hours by itself, and a monthly average can hide a short week.

## Why more links do not help

Adding a playlist, a repository or another folder only spreads the search out. None of them tells the reviewer which result to inspect. The fix is to put the exact item beside the claim it supports. The video organizes the material around three questions: what did you finish, what hours are documented, and what evidence can be opened. Each claim gets its own destination, and a missing item, such as an unplayable video, stays visibly unresolved.

## The executive summary

That arrangement is the executive summary: enough context to understand the work and enough evidence to check it. It includes:

- your project and group
- the current agreement and its end date
- the requested dates
- two or three results
- the next deliverables

Save it as Markdown inside your own fellows folder before it goes out.

## Sending the request

Email the report link to both addresses Monday to Wednesday during business hours, before your agreement expires. Renewals require approval, and agreements are not backdated. For OPT post-completion compliance, the video says to document 20 or more qualifying hours, but hours alone do not establish full compliance. Individual requirements go to your school's international office. The video is not legal or immigration advice.

## Key takeaways

- Write a short Markdown report in your own fellows folder rather than sending only a folder of files.
- Show completed work, actual weekly hours and a direct evidence link for each claim.
- Email the report link to both hr@humanitarians.ai and bear@humanitarians.ai.
- Send it Monday to Wednesday, business hours, before the agreement expires.
- Renewals require approval and agreements are not backdated.
- More links do not fix a missing connection between a claim and its evidence.
- Leave missing evidence visible instead of hiding it.
- Documented hours alone do not establish OPT compliance.
