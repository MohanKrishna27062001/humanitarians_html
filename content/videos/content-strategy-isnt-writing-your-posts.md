Someone hears "content strategy" and assumes Claude will hand back finished social posts or a content calendar. The video corrects that. ## What the skill actually reads

The skill works from actual sales data pulled from QuickBooks, PayPal, or Square. Before any post gets written, it ranks products into top performers, slow movers, and trending items. The video's framing is that strategy comes before copy: knowing what is working with real numbers is the starting point, not a side effect of writing.

## A skill is a folder

## How it runs

The steps run in order. The skill pulls 90 days of sales data from QuickBooks, PayPal, or Square, ranks it against seasonality, and hands back a 30-day brief. Nothing skips ahead. The owner approves the brief before anything gets built from it.

## Six sections, then it stops

The brief has six parts:

- an executive summary
- what to push hard
- what to hold steady
- what to reposition or pause
- seasonal opportunities coming up
- recommended offers

That is the entire output. There is no calendar, no captions, and no images. Those wait for the owner's approval and a separate skill.
