## Your own numbers, not the news

The brief is made of your own numbers: revenue against last week, top sellers, wins and watches, over the past seven or fourteen days. The video states plainly that it does not track outside market or industry news, pull an RSS feed or search the web. Its input is the business's own sales records.

## A skill is a folder

The brief is built from a skill, which the video describes as a folder Claude reads before it works. Inside is one file written in plain language that spells out exactly what this weekly check covers. Claude reads the file and then follows it, which is why the video says the file is the whole program.

## How it runs

The steps run in order. Claude reads the file, works through each step exactly as written, and returns the result. Nothing runs out of sequence unless a step itself says so.

## Exactly four fields

For this skill, the weekly pulse covers exactly four things.

- Revenue measured against the week before.
- Top sellers.
- Wins.
- Watches, meaning items worth keeping an eye on.

The look-back window can widen from seven days to fourteen, but that is the only adjustable setting. If you ask the skill about anything outside those four things, there is no mode that covers it. The video presents this boundary as a fact about how the skill is written, not as an argument, and it does not claim that the skill replaces a manager's own weekly review.
