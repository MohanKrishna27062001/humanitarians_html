In the AI for Good program, the aim is to develop transparent, fair software solutions for real-world challenges. Lyrical Literacy brings music and AI together. The video presents it as a program that supports brain health and language acquisition, drawing on neuroscience and song. Researchers are mentored as they remix learning and create songbooks and apps. The video says the program harmonizes data with development, and offers the line that music is medicine too.

## Botspeak

Botspeak is about teaching people to talk with machines effectively, ethically and clearly, from novices to experts. The video describes it as bridging the gap between people and large language models. Through workshops and videos, it shows that language is the key to using AI well. Prompt by prompt, the idea is to democratize access to AI's full potential.
